About Global Policy Network

We are a globally focused, independent policy institute driving positive societal change through evidence-based advocacy, strategic partnerships, and innovative solutions. Our mission is to shape policies that improve outcomes in health, education, and sustainability, while strengthening collaboration between the public and private sectors.

We combine thought leadership, independent research, and stakeholder mobilisation to turn insight into action and close the gap between evidence and implementation. Through thought leadership, webinars, and roundtables, we create platforms that spark dialogue, generate insight, and unlock collaboration.

Our consultancy arm delivers practical impact through workshops, training, strategy development, and network analysis, helping organisations navigate complexity and deliver results. As a socially responsible organisation, we reinvest a portion of our profits into philanthropic causes, reinforcing our commitment to lasting positive change.

Our Mission

Our mission is to ensure that innovative policies are also scalable and sustainable. Aligned with SDG 3 and SDG 17, we work to create global impact through research, reform, and strategic collaboration.

Global Policy Network empowers decision-makers to adopt meaningful solutions that address real-world challenges and drive effective, equitable change.
