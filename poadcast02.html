About Us

Driving Policy Forward

We are a globally focused, independent policy institute driving positive societal change through evidence-based advocacy, strategic partnerships, and innovative solutions. Our mission is to shape policies that improve outcomes in health, education, and sustainability.

Our Mission Meet the Team
Our Mission

Creating Positive Societal Impact

We combine thought leadership, independent research, and stakeholder mobilisation to turn insight into action and close the gap between evidence and implementation.

Health

Shaping policies that improve health outcomes through evidence-based advocacy, medicines optimisation, primary care transformation, and equitable access.

Education

Supporting inclusive and innovative education policies that empower learners and strengthen systems through research and cross-sector collaboration.

Sustainability

Championing sustainable development through policy innovation, strategic partnerships, and practical solutions aligned with global goals.

Integrity

We act with honesty, transparency, and respect in all our engagements.

Innovation

We champion creative and evidence-based approaches to global challenges.

Collaboration

We connect people, ideas, and solutions across sectors and borders.
